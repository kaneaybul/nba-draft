# November 17–19 shared-minutes replay

The replay used the two published player rosters and team schedules, dated injury outlooks, completed-season comparison data, the November 17 native annual contract rows and market curve, and the November 19 continuation's impact knots, replacement levels, position shares and league production denominator.

All 524 continuing players had reconciled four-year minutes. Two players entered the roster population and one left. There were no transfers between the continuing players in this interval.

For the mature-player dollar replay, November 17 signed terms and the completed-season market curve were carried forward. For 328 continuing players, applying those terms and option decisions to each date's original raw production reproduced every released annual production and asset cell and the resulting released four-year quote within half a cent. These players were then repriced using the shared forecast on each date. Draft-window quotes require the separate year-four appraisal and are outside this replay.
