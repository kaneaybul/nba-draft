# Year-four development accounting

During the first four years, the player's production stock is one shared
on-pace estimate of the appraisal at the end of year four. Both sides of the
blend price seasons five through eight from the year-four assessment date:

`padded production = (1 - NBA weight) * original expectation + NBA weight * updated endpoint appraisal`

The original expectation is the frozen draft-slot prior, using only labels
completed before the draft. The updated appraisal prices each endpoint revision
scenario before taking its expectation. Early-career service in the current
rolling window is not substituted for this mature endpoint. Available direct
future-season minutes inform both calibration forecasts and live extensions.

NBA weight uses verified cumulative minutes and an evidence scale fitted against
chronologically completed year-four labels. Offseason observations of a
completed season do not add its minutes again. In the final development season,
remaining prior mass also shrinks with remaining season exposure. Endpoint
revision uncertainty converges to zero at the same boundary. At year four,
production and the full asset quote converge to the ordinary model. Rookie
in-season weighting applies the completed-season evidence rule; it is not a
separately validated rookie-month fit. No cohort or league total is centered.

The full asset price adds the existing rolling service/contract adjustment to
this padded production stock. Independent endpoint-revision and current
service-cost scenario pools preserve their respective marginal distributions;
the reported interim asset quantiles use their independent product, not an
invented pairing of empirical careers.

Trades transfer that same full asset price. Draftee Development before the
transfer belongs to the seller; later changes belong to the buyer until the
player's original year-four boundary. The original expectation and development
clock never reset. After that boundary, Aging and Player Development apply.

A contract counterfactual may change ordinary continuation assumptions, but its
production component cannot be subtracted from an unchanged padded endpoint.
For a padded player, only its financial component enters Contract Management.
This prevents two-way conversions and similar transactions from generating
offsetting development losses and contract gains.

Version v11 reconstructs the full observed history and all shared value exports,
retaining v10. When refitting changes endpoint priors, historical pick portfolios
are repriced from their dated legal rights and archived forecasts as well.
